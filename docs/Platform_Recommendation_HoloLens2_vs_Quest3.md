# Platform Recommendation: HoloLens 2 → Meta Quest 3

**Document Purpose:** Platform selection justification for thesis project  
**Date:** January 22, 2026  
**Recommendation:** Migrate from Microsoft HoloLens 2 to Meta Quest 3

---

## Executive Summary

This document presents the rationale for selecting **Meta Quest 3** over **Microsoft HoloLens 2** as the target platform for the XR First Responder Training System thesis project. The recommendation is based on platform viability, development sustainability, cost-effectiveness, and long-term research impact.

---

## 1. Critical Issue: HoloLens 2 End of Production

| Factor | HoloLens 2 Status | Impact on Thesis |
|--------|-------------------|------------------|
| **Production** | ❌ **Discontinued (December 2024)** | Cannot acquire additional units for scaling |
| **Successor** | ❌ No announced successor | Technology dead-end |
| **Microsoft Strategy** | Shifted focus to Meta partnership | Validates Quest platform |
| **Enterprise Support** | Maintenance mode only | Declining resources and updates |
| **SDK Updates** | Minimal updates expected | Risk of compatibility issues |

**Source:** Microsoft announced in late 2024 that HoloLens 2 production has ceased, with no HoloLens 3 planned. Microsoft is now partnering with Meta for enterprise XR solutions.
