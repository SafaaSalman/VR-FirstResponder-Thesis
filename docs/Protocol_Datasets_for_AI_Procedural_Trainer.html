Key Insight: 32 of 56 identified sources (57%) are US Government public domain — freely usable without licensing restrictions. The three most valuable immediate assets are the JavaScript-based expert systems (CHEMM-IST, REMM, WISER) which contain pre-built, extractable decision logic that can be directly integrated into a VR training AI.
Critical Gap: Despite 56 sources identified, zero provide emergency response protocols in a standardized machine-readable format designed for AI consumption. Every source requires manual or semi-automated conversion. The proposed solution — LLM-assisted extraction into HL7 FHIR PlanDefinition format — would create the first open-source machine-readable emergency protocol repository, a significant thesis contribution.
